# Sales or technology?

It all comes down to moats.

Focusing on sales and disregarding technology means that you are not creating something long term, as sales people see technology as just another product.

Forcusing on technology without sales would just kill the business immediately.

# Market or solution?

You need both. VC / successful people like to say market first because of conflict of interest; they care more about market, not specific ways of solving it.

However, as founder, you need to care about solution, because you need to find a solution easy to you but difficult for others. Therefore, a lot of successful founders started with solutions and find a market. This is subject to heavy survivor bias and is actually not a good strategy, but it shows that starting from markets may not be as feasible, providing you don't have the "passion".

Ergo, you need both to succeed. It's best if you can plan for both prior to starting.
