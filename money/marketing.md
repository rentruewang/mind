# Marketing

On sites like reddit, you need to spell out what you mean to reduce people's cognitive load.

Do not make assumption of what they know.

Give them full context of your inference (no stretching), e.g. from [this](https://www.reddit.com/r/browsers/comments/1uo30by/comment/ovovzbs/) to [this](https://www.reddit.com/r/browsers/comments/1uo30by/comment/ovp8bb7/), forking itself does not cost much, but the implied development does.

Think in terms of what they know (Carnegie how to win friends and influence people),
and create something of interest.

### Concrete

A concrete example is better than purely thoery crafting.

Concrete is easier to convince readers (see stories "show don't tell" section),
because the state / transitions are easier to accept and less "barriers" for readers.

### Examples

When explaining stuff, present them with final results.

You can go against the tides by prefixing saying "I agree with you, but...". Way better received.

## Think in terms of medium

Think of how the user sees your content.

For example, on reddit, when they suddenly bump another comment (which echos your comment) to the top, you need to add something else, otherwise, other users won't think of your comment as novel even if it did come earlier.
