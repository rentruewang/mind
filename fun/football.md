# Football

Both CR7 and Messi are good at dribbling (when they dribble), but with different styles.

## Dribbling

Messi is fast in acceleration (including acceleration, decceleration, turning), which is why he is good at tight spaces. He also keeps the ball close.

CR7 is fast in top speed which is why his dribbling has more "sending balls off to open space + sprint". He loses more speed when doing the "Messi" style (tiny frequent steps) dribbling in tight spaces, partially because he is normally faster (therefore more affected), partially because of his taller frame.

Neymar is not fast. He uses tricks and fakes. Works in tight spaces but not great in open.
