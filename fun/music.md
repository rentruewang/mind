# Classical music

Development = take a melody (like a story character), then transform / vary it. This makes a "thread".

Progression is based on development, leading towards a climax.

E.g. Chopin is better at small pieces because he doesn't develop too much (his ballades have more development).

2 themes can connect together by complimenting each other in counterpoint (you brain repeats the previous theme, coupled with the next it sounds even better).

## Climax

Similar to story climax (unified event):

1. Threads merge: different melodies (story lines) are now weaved together at the same time.
2. Character density: Every instrument plays, maybe faster pacing etc.
